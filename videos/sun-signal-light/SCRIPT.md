# The Light We Lost — Sun Signal, 30s

Thesis: humans ran on sunlight for millennia; artificial light scrambled the signal; Sun Signal gives it back.
Style: whimsical hand-drawn collage — torn warm paper, cut-outs, pencil/ink linework, grain, stop-motion boil (12fps).

| # | Narration | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | For hundreds of thousands of years, humans followed the sun. | Torn-paper sun rises over layered paper hills; cut-out figures (cave fire, farmer, villager) slide in across ages. |
| 2 | Its light told our bodies when to wake, work, rest and sleep. | Sun travels a pencil arc; figure poses swap on each verb (wake/work/rest/sleep). |
| 3 | Then we made our own light. | Paper bulb slams in, sun gets papered over. |
| 4 | Too bright at midnight. Too dim at noon. Different everywhere we go. | Bulbs, streetlamp, phone glow pop in clashing; kinetic words. |
| 5 | And our rhythm lost its signal. | Pencil rhythm line tangles into scribble; tired figure. |
| 6 | Sun Signal gives it back: your sun, your day, where you are. | Paper tears open, sun returns, arc traces into the Sun Signal clock; title. |

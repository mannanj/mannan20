### Task 274: Explore a quantified-life page about the many ways I have existed, acted, and been experienced

Create a concept for a personal page that presents an unusual quantitative
self-portrait: counts and estimates of the ways my work, identity, body, and life
have been encountered or expressed. Preserve the ambiguity of the idea for now.
The final route, scope, categories, collection methods, and visual treatment are
deliberately undecided.

#### Core idea

- [ ] Explore a public page that collects interesting lifetime and ongoing counts
      about me.
- [ ] Treat the page as a perspective on a person rather than a conventional
      analytics dashboard.
- [ ] Consider `/views`, `/links`, `/counts`, or a broader personal ledger as the
      route and organizing concept.
- [ ] Leave room for exact measurements, imported platform statistics, manual
      records, and clearly labeled estimates or extrapolations.
- [ ] Decide later which counts are meaningful enough to publish and how they
      should be grouped.

#### Possible things to count

- [ ] Every link on my site that has been shared or can be accessed, with view and
      visit counts where those can be measured.
- [ ] My total posts or published artifacts across platforms such as Twitter/X,
      Facebook, YouTube, and other networks.
- [ ] Individual media totals, including YouTube videos and their views.
- [ ] The number of times people may have looked at me in real life.
- [ ] Real-life attention broken down by event, place, period, or context where
      that can be responsibly estimated.
- [ ] Counts of things I have done or experienced, such as how many times I have
      eaten steak and how many hours I have slept.
- [ ] Other meaningful quantities discovered later: things made, conversations,
      journeys, places, meals, words, days, habits, or recurring experiences.

#### Open questions

- [ ] Determine whether this is primarily about being **viewed**, about everything
      I have **done**, or about the broader idea of a life expressed through
      numbers.
- [ ] Determine whether `/links` is the whole experience, one section of
      `/views`, or one category within a larger `/counts` page.
- [ ] Determine which values should be live, periodically synchronized, manually
      entered, reconstructed from history, or estimated.
- [ ] Determine how to distinguish exact, incomplete, inferred, and playful
      numbers so the page stays honest without losing its intrigue.
- [ ] Determine whether the experience should emphasize a single grand total, a
      collection of categories, a timeline, individual events, or some mixture.
- [ ] Determine what should remain private even if it can technically be counted.

#### Notes and interpretation

The strongest version may be broader than `/links` or `/views`: a `/counts` page
that acts as a quantitative autobiography. “Views” could still be a central
category, spanning views of things I made, visits to places I control, and moments
when another person looked at me. Other categories could show actions and lived
time. Together, these numbers could reveal the scale and texture of an ordinary
life in a way that prose cannot.

The page should not imply false precision. Every number could carry a provenance
or confidence label such as **measured**, **platform-reported**, **manually
recorded**, or **estimated**. Estimates may be interesting precisely because they
make the limits of self-measurement visible.

Camera-based estimation of real-world attention needs special care. A future
design should assess consent, bystander privacy, local recording and biometric
laws, data retention, and whether the same artistic point can be achieved without
storing identifiable faces or raw footage. A safer direction may be on-device,
ephemeral detection that retains only anonymous aggregate counts. Looking toward
a camera also does not necessarily mean looking at me, so these figures should be
presented as estimates rather than facts.

This task is exploratory. Do not lock in a name, route, information architecture,
technical implementation, or promise that every possible count will be collected
until the concept is revisited and narrowed.

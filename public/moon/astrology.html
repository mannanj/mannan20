Method

Each reading is built from the sky at the exact instant of the lunation (JPL DE421 for the instant and eclipse geometry, Swiss Ephemeris for planetary longitudes; both agree to 0.0001°). Nothing is written per date by hand; a sentence appears only when the factor that justifies it was computed.

  1. Sign and degree. The Moon's tropical longitude at the New or Full Moon gives the sign, the degree (printed to the arc-minute) and the decan. 29° or later is flagged as the last degree.
  2. Aspects, tightest first. Conjunction, sextile, square, trine, quincunx and opposition from the lunation to Mercury–Pluto and Chiron, within the orbs in the table below. At a Full Moon each planet is measured from the Moon and from the Sun and the tighter side is kept (a planet opposite the Moon "sits with the Sun"). Chiron is capped at 3°. The reading names up to four.
  3. Ruler. The sign's modern ruler, with the traditional ruler noted where it differs (Scorpio, Aquarius, Pisces): its sign, whether it is retrograde, and whether it aspects the lunation itself.
  4. Retrogrades. Mercury, Venus and Mars whenever retrograde; outer planets only when they rule the sign or aspect the lunation.
  5. Eclipse and node. Whether the ephemeris found a solar or lunar eclipse, its type, the distance to the nearer lunar node and which node (North: growth; South: release).
  6. Timing. New Moon: intentions run to the next New Moon. Full Moon: strongest ±2–3 days, resolving over two weeks. Eclipse: about six months.

Code: astrology-app/api/astrology_engine/lunation.py and western_math.py (profile western-tropical-v1); the moon generator calls it from sun/data/moon/lunation_reading.py. Write-up: astrology-app/docs/lunation-readings/.

Sources

Method and orbs were drawn from how these publish their lunation readings; full notes with quotes and access dates are in docs/lunation-readings/research-notes.md.

  • Cafe Astrology — dated New/Full Moon event pages: sign, degree, aspects ("aligns", "harmonizes", "squares"), ruler, eclipse node, "the energy is strongest…" timing.
  • Astrology King (Jamie Partridge) — aspects with orbs, quincunxes, fixed stars; New Moon influence lasts to the next lunation, eclipses about six months.
  • AstroStyle — sign themes, retrogrades, eclipse node framing.
  • Chani Nicholas — traditional rulers (Mars for Scorpio, Jupiter for Pisces), two-week editorial focus.
  • Astrodienst Astrowiki — orb conventions; Kepler College — applying aspects are stronger.
  • Bernadette Brady, Predictive Astrology: The Eagle and the Lark — a lunation within ~19° of a node belongs to eclipse season; Jean Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms ch. 54 — eclipse certain below 15.35° (solar) / 9.5° (lunar) of the node, impossible beyond 18.5° / 12.3°.
  • Farmers' Almanac — North Node eclipses pull forward, South Node eclipses release.

Workbook: how to read a lunation

  1. Find the instant and the degree. Read the Moon's sign and degree. Early degrees mean the theme is starting; the last degree (29°) means something is finishing. The sign tells you the arena (see the sign table): a New Moon seeds it, a Full Moon brings it to a head.
  2. List the aspects, tightest first. Anything within orb counts; under 1° dominates the reading. Hard aspects (conjunction, square, opposition, quincunx) add friction or focus; soft aspects (trine, sextile) add support. The planet says what kind: Saturn limits, Uranus surprises, Neptune blurs, Pluto intensifies, Jupiter expands, Mars drives, Venus relates, Mercury talks, Chiron heals.
  3. Check the ruler. The sign's ruler shows how the theme will actually play out: its sign colours it, retrograde slows it, an aspect to the lunation ties it in directly.
  4. Note retrogrades. Mercury retrograde: review rather than commit. Venus: relationships and money revisit the past. Mars: energy turns inward. Outer-planet retrogrades last months and matter only when the planet is involved.
  5. Is it an eclipse? A lunation within about 15° of a node (solar) or 9.5° (lunar) is one. North Node: a push toward new ground; South Node: a clearing. Eclipse effects run about six months, not two weeks.
  6. Set the timing. New Moon intentions in the first day or two, running to the next New Moon; Full Moon peaks for two or three days and resolves over two weeks.

Decisions

  • Tropical zodiac. The moon page and every compared publisher use the tropical zodiac, so these readings do too, even though Mannan's astrology engine is otherwise Vedic (sidereal, Lahiri). The tropical profile western-tropical-v1 was added beside vedic-lahiri-v1; the Vedic profile is unchanged.
  • Modern rulers first. Of the compared sites that name a ruler, Cafe Astrology, Astrology King, Elsa Elsa and AstroStyle use the modern ruler (Uranus for Aquarius, Neptune for Pisces); Chani uses the traditional one. Readings name the modern ruler and add the traditional one in brackets.
  • Orbs. 8° conjunction and opposition, 6° square and trine, 4° sextile, 2° quincunx, Chiron capped at 3°. Chosen to match what the corpus names (an opposition at 6.5° and quincunxes near 1° were named; a Mercury conjunction at 8.1° was named once and is the known miss).
  • Eclipse fact from the ephemeris. The generator passes the computed eclipse (or "none") so the reading never infers one from node distance alone.

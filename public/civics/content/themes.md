# Focus, Themes & Pattern Hypotheses

## Focus: Civic participation under survival demands

People facing survival demands may find it unclear what matters to them or whether civic options, if any, fit their circumstances and can plausibly influence outcomes they care about.

Provisional synthesis, not a source finding.

# Themes

## Meaning

Interpretation: participation may be difficult to judge when people lack room to clarify priorities or understand the available choice. This is a provisional synthesis.

## Practical Fit

Interpretation: an option may need to fit real capacity and be legible enough to assess before commitment. This is a provisional synthesis.

## Plausible Influence

Interpretation: people may need a plausible account of how participation could matter in order to judge an option. This is a provisional synthesis, not evidence of impact.

# Pattern hypotheses

- **Values may require reflective space.** Hypothesis: survival demands may reduce room to clarify what matters before choosing how to participate. This is an interpretation of S2 and S3, not a causal finding.
- **Participation must fit real capacity.** Hypothesis: work, care, survival tasks, schedules, and duration demands may make some participation options infeasible. This is an interpretation of S3 and S4, not a causal finding.
- **Options may be hard to judge before commitment.** Hypothesis: people may need enough detail about demands and possible impact to judge an option before committing scarce time. This is an interpretation of S1 and S4, not a causal finding.
- **Plausible influence remains uncertain.** Hypothesis: unclear impact or influence may affect whether participation appears worthwhile. This is an interpretation of S1 and S4, not a causal finding.

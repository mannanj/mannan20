# What Andrew Huberman Didn't Say

Andrew Huberman says light is the most important thing to change, if you could change just one thing.

What Andrew Huberman didn't say, is we used to follow the sun for millennia before introducing alarm clocks, artificial lights and night-time stimulation that takes over the nervous system. He's aware of the difficulty most people will have controlling noise and light pollution in their home environment, as not everyone has the capability or luxury to control such things, some people self impose them on themselves, and he decides to be sensitive and adapt the message for most people. This makes the message less true. If you instead "Wake up to sunlight" and turn off artificial light after sunset, you don't have to do any of the difficult willpower centric stuff of deciding when to sleep and turning off the light. It just happens automatically. sunsignal.app (https://sunsignal.app) tests this hypothesis and provides a clock UI to help out.

If you can bring the sunrise into your room linked to a light, try that for a single day and see an immediate result: you wake up to light not alarms or clock time. Have no blue light at night? You naturally want to fall asleep and sleep more in the winter and less in the summer.

You don't follow industry, your biology follows nature. Industry can mask and hack that, but it hasn't conquered it yet.

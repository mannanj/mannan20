Timer.dmg - how it is encrypted and how to open it

ALGORITHM
  Apple encrypted disk image, made with:
    hdiutil create -encryption AES-256 -format UDZO
  Cipher: AES-256. The key is derived from a password, which is not stored here.
  File names and contents are all encrypted.

TO DECRYPT AND INSTALL (any Mac, built-in tools only)
  curl -LO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mannanj/mannan20/main/timer/Timer.dmg
  hdiutil attach Timer.dmg          (asks for the password)
  /Volumes/Timer/install.sh
  hdiutil detach /Volumes/Timer

  Or double-click Timer.dmg in Finder and enter the password.

  Needs Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or later. No admin rights needed.
  To uninstall: /Volumes/Timer/uninstall.sh
